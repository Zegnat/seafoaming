Povy is the human that most characters talk to. In the [SempAi biographies](https://supercabezon4288.wixsite.com/seafoam-kitten/sempai-bios-canon), the Kitonian aliens of [Zelenia](@/characters/zelenia.md) watch Povy closely, because they think that the genetics of Povy are important for their plans. Povy can possibly not die, and [Ms. Spooky](@/characters/ms-spooky.md) protects the soul of Povy.

Povy has many relations: a best friend ([Kiwi](@/characters/kiwi.md)), a roommate ([Xak](@/characters/xak.md)), an attic roommate ([Kiki](@/characters/kiki.md)), a maid ([Mai](@/characters/mai.md)) and a mother ([Bitchy Mom](@/characters/bitchy-mom.md)).
