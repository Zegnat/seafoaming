Zelenia is the empress of the Kitonians. She plans to take over Earth and make it a resort. Only her secret clan, the Crescent Guard, knows the full plan. Her younger sister [Fay](@/characters/fay.md) is not part of it.

Zelenia thinks that most humans are disgusting. She abducts and probes [Povy](@/characters/povy.md) regularly, because Povy is necessary for her plan. She agrees to most demands of Fay and limits the pain that humans get in her experiments.
