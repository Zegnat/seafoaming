Zelenia is an alien with a mean streak. In her videos she takes the listener onto her space ship to run tests, probes and exams, and she does not hide what she thinks of humans. Her sister is [Fay](@/characters/fay.md), and the two sisters have a deal that limits what Zelenia can do to her test subjects.
