How far the transcripts of the videos are. Pick a video, download its transcript as a WebVTT file from the video page, correct it, and add it to the [community folder](https://github.com/Zegnat/seafoaming/tree/main/community) as `videos/<id>.vtt`. The [community README](https://github.com/Zegnat/seafoaming/blob/main/community/README.md) explains how. To correct a file in the browser, you can use the [VTT editor](https://zegnat.github.io/zegnat-vtt-editor/).
