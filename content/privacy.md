This site does not track you. It sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, and has no analytics and no advertising.

## Files of this site

All files that a page needs come from this site itself: the pages, the stylesheet, the scripts, the icons and the video thumbnails. A page loads nothing from other websites until you use the video player.

[GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/) hosts the site. When your browser requests a page, GitHub receives your IP address, like every web server does. The [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) explains how GitHub handles this data.

## The video player

A video page shows a thumbnail from this site in place of the player. The site does not contact YouTube while you only look at the page.

When you start the video, or click a chapter or transcript time, the page loads the player from YouTube. From then on, YouTube receives data from your browser, and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies.

- In most browsers, the player comes from `www.youtube-nocookie.com`, the [privacy-enhanced mode](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171780) of YouTube. YouTube says that in this mode, the videos that you watch do not influence what YouTube shows you later.
- In Safari and in browsers on phones and tablets, the player comes from `www.youtube.com`. These browsers block videos that start by themselves, so the site needs the YouTube player API there, which does not use the privacy-enhanced mode.

## Links to other websites

Some links lead to other websites, for example to YouTube, to the social media of Seafoam Kitten, or to GitHub to edit a page. When you follow such a link, the privacy policy of that website applies.
